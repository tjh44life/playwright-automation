# Requirements

## Business goals
- Standardize Sire & Sons Smokehouse operations across locations.
- Build repeatable systems that can scale into franchises.
- Protect margins with inventory, labor, and financial controls.
- Use AI agents to automate monitoring and decision support.
- Create a parent group structure for future brands under Sire & Sons Collective.

## Core modules
- Operations: line checks, SOPs, opening/closing tasks
- Training: onboarding, certification, skill tracking
- Inventory: ordering, vendor management, waste tracking
- Finance: prime cost analysis, reporting, forecasting
- Marketing: local promotions and review response
- Onboarding: franchise setup and track-to-open workflows
