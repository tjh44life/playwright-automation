# Branding Direction

## Restaurant Name
Sire & Sons Smokehouse

## Brand Positioning
A modern smokehouse brand rooted in heritage, hospitality, and high-quality craftsmanship.

## Naming Convention for Future Brands
Use a parent group name that can house multiple restaurant concepts with distinct identities.

### Recommended Parent Group Name
Sire & Sons Collective

### Why this works
- It carries the founder identity from the flagship brand.
- It feels scalable for future concepts.
- It suggests a family of brands rather than a single restaurant.

## Brand Architecture Options

### Option 1: Sire & Sons Collective
Parent company / holding group for multiple restaurants and hospitality concepts.

### Option 2: Sire & Sons Hospitality Group
Better if the plan includes catering, events, and broader hospitality services.

### Option 3: Sire & Sons Brands
Simple and flexible for future sub-brands and franchise concepts.

## Recommended Direction
Use:
- Sire & Sons Smokehouse for the flagship restaurant
- Sire & Sons Collective as the umbrella group for future brands
