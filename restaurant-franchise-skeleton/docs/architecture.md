# Architecture Overview

## High-level layers
- Presentation: admin dashboard, manager tools, franchise portals
- Application: workflows for operations, training, inventory, finance, marketing
- Integration: POS, inventory, accounting, LMS, messaging, and AI services
- AI agents: margin watchdog, compliance supervisor, growth orchestrator
