# Naming Convention for Future Brands

## Core principle
Each future brand should feel distinct, memorable, and aligned with the parent group identity.

## Recommended structure
- Flagship restaurant: Sire & Sons Smokehouse
- Parent group: Sire & Sons Collective
- Future brands: [Name] by Sire & Sons

## Examples
- Sire & Sons Smokehouse
- Sire & Sons Bakery
- Sire & Sons Coffee
- Sire & Sons Grill
- Sire & Sons Taco House

## Naming rules
- Keep names easy to say and easy to remember.
- Make each concept feel like part of a family without being identical.
- Choose names that can scale into franchising and licensing.
